## **About Me**
You see **"Emissions Trading"** and **"Energy-Climate Policy Modelling"** on my profile, and yeah, that's a big part of what I do. But what that really means is I'm constantly digging into data, building models (lots of love for **MESSAGEix!**), and trying to make sense of how we can transition to a cleaner energy future. It's not just about the numbers, though. It's about understanding the real-world implications and finding pathways that are both *effective* and *fair*.
